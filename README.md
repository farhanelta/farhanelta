## Who I Am

I'm a Computer Science student at **Bina Nusantara University (BINUS)** with an immersion in **Cybersecurity**, and a multidisciplinary designer who works across UI/UX, branding, motion, and 3D. I came up through Software Engineering at Telkom Vocational School, which means I think in systems before I think in pixels.

My work sits at the intersection of security and design — I care about building products that are both defensible and comprehensible. Currently contributing as a designer at **BNCC** and a member of **GDGoC**.
